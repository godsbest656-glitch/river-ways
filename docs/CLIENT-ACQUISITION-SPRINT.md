# River Ways Client Acquisition Sprint

**Owner:** River Ways  
**Start:** 13 October 2026  
**Primary objective:** Create qualified sales conversations and close a first/next paid project while the website launch proceeds in parallel.

## Operating principle

Do not wait for the website launch to begin selling. Use the current portfolio, a clear offer, and direct human outreach. The website is a credibility and conversion asset, not a prerequisite for prospecting.

## Initial offer

Lead with a specific business outcome, not a long list of services:

> River Ways helps businesses turn disconnected marketing, websites and search visibility into a clearer system for generating and converting qualified demand.

For first conversations, sell a small, well-scoped paid diagnostic or implementation sprint rather than a vague retainer:
- **Demand & Conversion Audit:** review positioning, discovery, website journey, calls to action and follow-up; deliver a prioritized action plan.
- **Website Conversion Sprint:** improve one high-value page or enquiry journey, with agreed acceptance criteria.
- **Search & AI Discovery Sprint:** assess technical/search foundations, content opportunities and discoverability; prioritize fixes.

Confirm scope, timeline and price against the actual prospect before quoting. Never promise guaranteed leads, rankings or revenue.

## Who to prioritize

Score prospects on observable evidence, not assumptions:
1. Clear commercial offer and an identifiable decision-maker.
2. An active business with a product/service worth selling.
3. Visible friction: unclear positioning, weak mobile journey, broken/hidden calls to action, thin search pages, outdated content, or no obvious enquiry path.
4. A likely reason to act now: launch, expansion, new service, hiring, campaign, seasonal demand or recent public announcement.
5. A credible way to contact them through a public business channel.

Start with a small batch of 20 researched prospects. Test no more than two segments in the first batch; compare reply quality before expanding. Suitable hypotheses to test include B2B/professional services and consumer businesses with high-value enquiries. Do not assume a segment is validated until conversations confirm it.

## Daily sales cadence

- Research 10 prospects and record a specific, verifiable observation for each.
- Send 5–10 personalized first-touch messages where contact details and channel use are appropriate.
- Follow up with previously contacted prospects when due.
- Ask for a short discovery conversation around the prospect's business outcome.
- Record every touch, reply, next action and date in the pipeline.
- Keep outreach relevant, lawful, honest and human-led; respect opt-outs and platform rules. Do not scrape private data or send bulk unsolicited messages.

Hi [Name] — bringing this back up in case improving [specific outcome] is on the list this quarter. The main opportunity I saw was [one useful observation]. Happy to send the concise recommendation here; no pressure if the timing is off.

### Discovery call structure (15–20 minutes)
1. What business outcome matters most over the next 90 days?
2. Where do qualified opportunities currently come from?
3. Where do people drop off between discovering the business and enquiring/buying?
4. What has already been tried, and what is the constraint?
5. If the problem were solved, what would improve and how would you measure it?
6. Reflect back the problem, recommend one appropriately scoped next step, and agree a date for the proposal/decision.

## Pipeline fields

Track: company, segment, website, decision-maker, public contact channel, evidence/observation, likely business impact, score, first-touch date, channel, status, last touch, next action, next-action date, estimated value, notes, opt-out.

Suggested statuses: Researching → Ready to contact → Contacted → Replied → Discovery booked → Qualified → Proposal sent → Won / Lost / Nurture / Do not contact.

## Weekly review

Measure activity and outcomes separately:
- researched prospects
- personalized first touches
- follow-ups completed
- positive replies
- discovery calls booked and held
- qualified opportunities
- proposals sent
- wins and collected revenue
- objections and repeated pain points

After the first 20–30 well-researched contacts, double down on the segment and message that produces relevant replies and real buying conversations. Do not optimize for message volume alone.
