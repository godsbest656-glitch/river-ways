## Qualification rubric (score each 0–2)

- **Fit:** clear service/product and commercial value.
- **Need evidence:** a specific, observable friction or missed opportunity.
- **Timing:** credible public trigger or reason to act soon.
- **Access:** relevant decision-maker and an appropriate public contact route.
- **Delivery fit:** River Ways can confidently deliver the proposed work.

Prioritize prospects scoring 7/10 or above only after the site review. These initial prospects are not yet qualified, and no contact should be sent until the personalization angle is verified.

## Outreach rule

Offer one specific insight and invite a brief conversation. Do not send a generic agency pitch. Do not imply access to private analytics or claim that the prospect is losing money without evidence. Respect opt-outs and applicable privacy/marketing rules.

## First test

Test two message angles across a small, manually researched batch:
1. **Conversion path:** help qualified visitors take the next step.
2. **Search/content journey:** connect high-intent discovery to relevant service pages and enquiries.

Track positive replies and qualified conversations, not just opens or sent messages. Keep prospect-specific notes in a controlled pipeline and avoid storing unnecessary personal data.
