# Privacy notice — River Ways website

## Enquiries

When you submit the project enquiry form, River Ways processes the name and email address you provide, plus any company, goal, and project context you choose to share. These details are used to respond to your request and manage a potential business relationship.

The form endpoint applies a short-window abuse-prevention check. It stores a hash of the connecting IP address, rather than the raw address, with the enquiry record. Enquiry storage is disabled until the Cloudflare environment has a strong random `RATE_LIMIT_SALT` secret configured (at least 32 characters). Do not include sensitive personal information, passwords, financial account details, or confidential third-party material in the form.

If configured, enquiry notifications are sent through Resend to River Ways' designated business inbox. The enquiry is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database configured by the site operator. Service providers process data under their own terms. An enquiry may be stored even if email notification fails, so the operator should review notification status and follow up from the saved record.

## Campaign attribution

If you submit an enquiry from a tagged campaign link, the campaign source, medium, campaign name, content, search term, and original landing-page path may be stored with that enquiry. This is first-party form attribution; it is not a claim that analytics or advertising pixels are active. The Growth Diagnostic processes assessment answers in your browser and does not send the answers or score to the server. Only details you choose to submit through the enquiry form are sent.

## Retention and requests

The site operator must configure and periodically review retention, deletion, access controls, and backup policies for the D1 database and notification provider. Contact akinsify0511@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of enquiry information, subject to applicable law.

## Analytics and third parties

This notice does not claim that analytics or social-listening services are active. If those services are added, this notice must be updated to explain the data collected, purposes, providers, and any consent choices required by applicable law.
