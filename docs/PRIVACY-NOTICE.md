# Privacy notice — River Ways website

## Enquiries

When you submit the project enquiry form, River Ways processes the name and email address you provide, plus any company, goal, and project context you choose to share. These details are used to respond to your request and manage a potential business relationship.

The form endpoint uses a short-window abuse-prevention check. It stores a hash of the connecting IP address, rather than the raw address, with the enquiry record. Configure a strong random `RATE_LIMIT_SALT` secret in Cloudflare Pages to make the hash harder to correlate across systems. Do not include sensitive personal information or confidential client material in the form.

If configured, enquiry notifications are sent through Resend to River Ways' designated business inbox. The enquiry is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database configured by the site operator. Service providers process data under their own terms.

## Retention and requests

The site operator must configure and periodically review retention, deletion, access controls, and backup policies for the D1 database and notification provider. Contact oluwafemi@riverwayse.com to request access, correction, or deletion of enquiry information, subject to applicable law.

## Analytics and third parties

This notice does not claim that analytics or social-listening services are active. If those services are added, this notice must be updated to explain the data collected, purposes, providers, and any consent choices required by applicable law.
