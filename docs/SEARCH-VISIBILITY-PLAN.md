# River Ways Search Visibility Implementation

## Target positioning
River Ways is a demand and growth partner providing demand generation, search engine optimization and AI discovery, conversion-focused websites, performance marketing, content and creative systems, and demand intelligence.

## Search intent to serve
Prioritize useful pages around:
- digital marketing agency in Nigeria
- SEO services in Nigeria
- website design and conversion optimization
- demand generation and go-to-market strategy
- performance marketing and paid acquisition
- content strategy and creative systems
- AI search visibility and generative engine optimization

Treat these as research hypotheses. Validate language and demand with Search Console, keyword research, sales conversations and actual customer wording. Do not keyword-stuff or create thin pages solely to target phrases.

## Technical foundations
- Keep the canonical hostname consistent with the live production domain.
- Make sure robots.txt allows crawling and the sitemap lists only canonical, indexable URLs.
- Verify the site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Submit the XML sitemap, inspect the homepage URL and request indexing after production launch.
- Check mobile rendering, page speed, image delivery, metadata, internal links, accessibility and HTTPS.
- Keep Organization structured data accurate and consistent with visible page content. Validate it with Google's Rich Results Test and Schema Markup Validator.
- Add dedicated, genuinely useful service pages with unique titles, descriptions, examples, process, FAQs and clear next steps; link them from the homepage.
- Add case studies only when the work and outcomes are real and permission is secured. No fabricated testimonials, client logos, awards or results.
- Add a useful insights section answering specific buyer questions, informed by real prospect conversations.
- Add descriptive alt text to meaningful imagery. Decorative images should have empty alt text. Compress and size images responsively.
- Prefer self-hosted optimized video or a reputable hosted embed; no autoplay with sound. Use a poster image, captions where speech exists, and reduced-motion/accessibility alternatives.

## Images and video
Use licensed assets from Pexels, Unsplash and Pixabay, verifying the current license and any third-party rights for each chosen asset. Do not use stock imagery to imply a person is a River Ways employee/client or that a depicted company endorses River Ways. Keep a simple asset register: asset URL, creator, source, license checked date, local filename, alt text.

## Important limitation
SEO changes improve clarity and eligibility; they cannot guarantee ranking, indexing, AI citations or a specific timeline. Indexing and visibility must be measured after launch.
