# Avenize — River Ways client engagement

**Status:** Execution foundation established; client approvals and product verification remain required.
**Client:** Avenize | **Agency:** River Ways
**Starting market:** Nigeria, Lagos first | **Budget assumption:** ₦0 until approved.

## Mandate
River Ways develops and executes Avenize's brand activation, marketing, creative production, customer acquisition, sales enablement and strategic partnership programme. This is not limited to social media management or graphic design.

Avenize is the product and client brand. River Ways is the agency. Keep Avenize's product, data, credentials, commercial commitments and identity separate from River Ways' own agency positioning and acquisition engine.

**Promise:** “The state of your business in seconds.”
**Brand line:** “Operate. Grow. Lead.”

## Commercial outcomes
1. Establish a credible, recognisable premium Business Operating System brand.
2. Explain verified product value in ordinary business-owner language.
3. Generate qualified SME, founder, executive and business-owner opportunities.
4. Convert interest into demonstrations, registrations, activated users and paying customers.
5. Develop measurable distribution and commercial partnerships.
6. Establish a repeatable acquisition process and conversion-funded growth model.

## Workstreams
- Brand strategy, identity application and corporate communication.
- Creative production: campaign graphics, video, motion, photography direction and product visuals.
- Product marketing: verified use cases, walkthroughs and demonstrations.
- Digital distribution: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and WhatsApp Business.
- Sales enablement: corporate deck, product brochure, one-pagers, demo deck and follow-up materials.
- Partnerships: separate proposals for Niyi Adesanya and LIMBsimple; neither is an endorsement or confirmed relationship.
- Offline activation: Lagos workshops, executive demos, business communities, QR-led demonstrations and referrals.
- Growth operations: prospect research, qualification, CRM, follow-up, attribution and reporting.

## Target market
Start with Nigeria and Lagos. Priority segments: SME owners/directors; founders; professional services; agencies and consultancies; retail and distribution; growing technology companies; business advisers; and operations executives.

Expansion markets—Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, UK, US and Canada—are conditional on product readiness, support capacity and commercial capability.

## Non-negotiable safeguards
- Do not invent product features, customer stories, testimonials, endorsements, pricing or performance claims.
- Use genuine Avenize product screenshots and verified demonstrations.
- Distinguish available features from roadmap concepts.
- Confirm links, contacts, registration, pricing and CTAs before publication.
- Do not start paid advertising or incur subscriptions, media spend or other financial commitments without approval.
- Do not promise exclusivity, commissions, funding, partner rewards or financial terms on Avenize's behalf.
- Obtain client approval before publication or external commitments.
- Keep client records, access credentials, product data and deliverables in the Avenize client workspace.

## Acceptance principle
A deliverable is complete only when it has an owner, source/editable file, approved copy, final export, QA evidence, client approval and—where applicable—publication or delivery evidence. Count commercial outcomes, not just assets produced.

## Evidence boundary
The logo image supplied by the client is the visual source of truth. Preserve the exact image; do not redraw or replace it. The client brief is the source for engagement scope. Product functionality, existing assets, website URLs, pricing, contact details and product readiness still require direct verification.
