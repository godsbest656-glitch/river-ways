# Avenize — Brand Design System

**Status:** Provisional specification derived from the supplied logo image. Client approval required before external publication.
**Identity asset:** Use the exact supplied Avenize logo. Preserve geometry, proportions, transparent background and original colours.

## Brand foundation
- Product category: Business Operating System.
- Promise: **The state of your business in seconds.**
- Brand line: **Operate. Grow. Lead.**
- Desired perception: premium, globally relevant, clear, confident, intelligent and operationally disciplined.
- Creative character: cinematic, editorial, modern and human; restrained glass surfaces and dimensional details; authentic product interface imagery.

## Sampled logo palette
Values sampled from the supplied 850 × 850 transparent PNG. These are reference values, not yet an approved full UI token system.

| Token | Hex | Intended role |
|---|---|---|
| Avenize Lime | #D8F45B | Primary accent, key action and emphasis |
| Avenize Silver | #C4C9CC | Secondary identity colour, neutral surfaces and clarity |
| Avenize Gold | #C6A56A | Premium accent, highlights and achievement |
| Obsidian | #111111 | Primary dark canvas and high-contrast text |

The logo is not a generic black-and-neon identity. Silver and gold are integral to its visual signature. Do not omit them.

## Colour use
- Use Obsidian for deep backgrounds and premium editorial compositions.
- Use Lime for primary CTAs, active states, key numbers and focal emphasis.
- Use Silver for supporting surfaces, dividers, secondary panels and restrained typography.
- Use Gold as a deliberate premium accent, not a competing primary CTA colour.
- Maintain accessible contrast. Never assume sampled logo colours automatically pass text contrast requirements.
- Establish light and dark surface tokens only after contrast testing against actual text sizes and states.

## Typography
Typography is not established by the supplied logo image. Select a highly legible contemporary sans-serif family with a clear hierarchy for executive audiences. Verify font licensing and availability before shipping.

## Art direction
- Real business owners, founders, professionals and teams in credible environments.
- Authentic Avenize screens, safe demo data and real workflow demonstrations.
- Strong editorial composition, controlled lighting, considered negative space and restrained dimensionality.
- Show the human/business context before decorative technology motifs.
- Glassmorphism and neofuturistic details are supporting devices, not the main visual language.
- Use the exact Avenize mark consistently; never stretch, recolour, crop or reconstruct it without approval.

## Avoid
- Generic SaaS/AI templates and stock-photo clichés.
- Artificial-looking people or fabricated product screenshots.
- Overcrowded layouts, excessive gradients, icon grids and engineering-style decoration.
- Unsupported claims such as “all-in-one” or specific automation/finance/HR capabilities unless verified.
- Confusing Avenize with River Ways or presenting the product as an agency service.

## Asset handling
The original supplied PNG is the canonical logo reference. Keep it unchanged. Create separately named exports only for approved use cases. SVG is permitted only if supplied or approved as a true vector; do not trace the raster and present it as the original vector.

## Design acceptance
1. Correct logo and sampled colour use.
2. Clear audience, message and CTA.
3. Verified product claims and authentic interface imagery.
4. Legibility and responsive/platform-safe composition.
5. Contrast and accessibility review.
6. Correct contact details, destination URL and tracking.
7. Client approval before publishing.
