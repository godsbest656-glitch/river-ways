# Avenize × Niyi Adesanya — Executive strategic relationship concept

**Status:** Private discussion draft. Not an offer, endorsement, agreement or commitment.
**Prepared by:** River Ways Agency for Avenize review.
**Prospect:** Niyi Adesanya.
**Purpose:** Explore an executive-level relationship that can increase business-owner trust, access, education and qualified product conversations.

## 1. The opportunity

Avenize is positioning itself as a premium Business Operating System with the promise: **“The state of your business in seconds.”**

The opportunity is to explore whether an executive-level collaboration could help business leaders better understand operational visibility, coordination and disciplined growth, while introducing relevant audiences to the actual Avenize product.

This concept is based only on the proposed relationship scope supplied in the Avenize marketing brief. No assumptions are made about the prospect's existing views, audience size, credentials, commercial terms or willingness to participate.

## 2. Proposed collaboration areas

- **Executive perspective:** A moderated conversation or interview on the operational challenges of growing businesses.
- **Business transformation education:** A practical session on visibility, coordination and management discipline.
- **Introductions:** Permission-based introductions to relevant founders, executives and business communities.
- **Educational events:** Co-hosted executive roundtables, business-owner workshops or virtual sessions.
- **Product exploration:** A private Avenize demonstration, with feedback from an executive/business-owner perspective.

## 3. Mutual value hypothesis

**Potential value to Niyi Adesanya**
- Opportunity to contribute to practical business education.
- Participation in relevant executive conversations and learning events.
- Access to a useful product demonstration for independent evaluation.
- Agreed visibility in event and educational materials if the relationship proceeds.

**Potential value to Avenize**
- Credible executive-level dialogue and market learning.
- Introductions to relevant decision-makers where voluntarily offered.
- Qualified attendance at educational sessions.
- Product feedback and potential demonstration opportunities.

All benefits are proposed and subject to mutual agreement. No endorsement or public association is implied.

## 4. Suggested pilot

A single 60–75 minute executive session:

**Working title:** “Growth, Visibility and the Operating Discipline Behind Strong Businesses”

Format:
1. Welcome and context — 5 minutes.
2. Executive discussion on operational visibility — 20 minutes.
3. Practical business-owner questions — 15 minutes.
4. Guided Avenize demonstration using verified product workflows — 15 minutes.
5. Questions and opt-in follow-up — 10–20 minutes.

The session may be online or hosted by a partner at no cost. No venue, speaker fee, sponsorship, travel or production expenditure is assumed.

## 5. Proposed success measures

- Relevant decision-makers invited and attending.
- Opt-in attendees and qualified business-owner leads.
- Demonstrations requested and completed.
- Product registrations and activation where measurable.
- Prospect feedback and product objections.
- Follow-up meetings and commercial opportunities.
- Cost per qualified opportunity, with any direct costs disclosed.

Targets should be agreed after audience access and capacity are understood.

## 6. Suggested commercial models for discussion

No model is proposed as accepted. Options for a later conversation may include:
- One-off paid or sponsored educational engagement.
- Fixed-scope event collaboration.
- Mutually agreed referral arrangement with transparent attribution.
- Strategic advisory relationship with defined scope and compensation.
- Non-financial pilot followed by a review.

Any fee, commission, referral reward, exclusivity, use of name/image, data sharing or public announcement requires a separate written agreement approved by Avenize.
