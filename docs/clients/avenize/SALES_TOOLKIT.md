## 4. Corporate presentation — proposed 10-slide narrative

1. **Avenize:** The state of your business in seconds.
2. **The growth reality:** More customers, people, projects and decisions create more moving parts.
3. **The visibility challenge:** Why business owners need a clearer view of operations.
4. **Introducing Avenize:** A Business Operating System.
5. **How it works:** Actual product overview, using verified interface screenshots only.
6. **Verified workflows:** Demonstrate 2–3 current workflows; feature names and claims supplied by product owner.
7. **Who it is for:** Priority customer segments, subject to product fit validation.
8. **Getting started:** Confirmed access, onboarding and support process.
9. **Commercial information:** Approved pricing, plans and availability only.
10. **Next step:** Request a demonstration / agreed contact route.

## 5. Product demonstration deck — proposed 8-slide narrative

1. Prospect's business context and operational question.
2. Current challenge as described by the prospect.
3. Avenize product overview.
4. Verified workflow 1 — actual product capture and explanation.
5. Verified workflow 2 — actual product capture and explanation.
6. What the demonstration does and does not establish.
7. Questions, fit and implementation considerations.
8. Agreed next step and contact.

Do not fill slides 4–5 with invented interface mockups. If the product owner has not verified the workflows, leave them clearly marked as pending.

### Follow-up after interest

Use only for warm or permission-based contacts. Do not distribute in bulk without permission.

## 7. Sales conversation guide

1. What does the business do and how is it currently organised?
2. What has become more difficult to keep track of as the business has grown?
3. How do you currently get a picture of what is happening across the business?
4. Who else is involved in evaluating operational systems?
5. Would seeing a verified Avenize workflow help assess relevance?
6. What would need to be true for the product to be worth adopting?
7. Agree the next step, owner and date.

Listen before presenting. Do not assume a prospect needs every Avenize capability.

## 8. Proof and content dependencies

Before final deck/brochure approval, obtain:
- Verified product description and current feature inventory.
- Actual screenshots and demo access.
- Confirmed target customer fit and user roles.
- Pricing, availability and onboarding/support details.
- Official URL, registration/demo route and approved contact details.
- Approved customer evidence, if any; otherwise omit testimonials and logos.
