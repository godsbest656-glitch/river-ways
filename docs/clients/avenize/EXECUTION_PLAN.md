# Avenize — 30-day launch and growth execution plan

**Planning baseline:** ₦0 approved marketing spend.
**Market:** Nigeria first; Lagos first activation.
**Schedule:** Relative days from client kickoff and access readiness. Workstreams may overlap where dependencies allow.

## Gate 0 — Readiness
Before public claims or launch:
- Obtain access to current product, demo environment and verified feature inventory.
- Collect canonical logo and existing brand assets.
- Confirm website/domain, registration flow, demo booking/contact route and support process.
- Confirm pricing, availability, target customer fit and commercial approval owner.
- Establish consent-aware lead capture, CRM ownership and reporting definitions.
- Identify approved, conditional and prohibited claims.

**Exit:** Product and commercial facts can be demonstrated and communicated without invention.

## Week 1 — Foundation
- Audit existing Avenize website, product screens, marketing assets and social presence.
- Finalise positioning, audience messages and approved claims.
- Apply supplied identity to a concise brand system.
- Prepare corporate presentation and product brochure structures.
- Develop campaign 001 creative concept and content pillars.
- Set up lead tracking, source attribution and approval workflow.

**Deliverables:** Product/brand audit, message guide, design system, launch campaign brief, corporate deck draft, brochure draft and reporting baseline.

## Week 2 — Production
- Produce launch hero artwork and platform adaptations.
- Create business-problem/solution content series.
- Record authentic product walkthroughs and feature demonstrations.
- Produce business-owner flyer, SME brochure, executive deck, sales demo deck and WhatsApp introduction materials.
- Draft separate partnership proposals for Niyi Adesanya and LIMBsimple.
- Prepare landing-page copy and conversion path.
- Build first publishing calendar.

**Deliverables:** First launch creative batch, sales toolkit, demo content, partnership proposal drafts, landing-page copy and publishing calendar.

## Week 3 — Market activation
- Begin approved organic publishing.
- Start targeted, respectful business-owner outreach.
- Research and contact Lagos SMEs and relevant decision-makers.
- Approach partnership prospects with tailored proposals.
- Schedule qualified product demonstrations.
- Confirm workshop venue/host only where a no-cost or partner-supported arrangement exists.
- Prepare QR-linked registration and demo materials.

**Deliverables:** Published content evidence, prospect register, outreach log, demo calendar, partner discussions and activation run sheet.

## Week 4 — Conversion and optimisation
- Follow up on active prospects with next actions and dates.
- Conduct demonstrations and capture structured feedback.
- Track registrations, activation and paid conversion where available.
- Review partner pipeline and mutual-value outcomes.
- Evaluate which segments and messages generated qualified interest.
- Produce next-month campaign based on evidence.

**Deliverables:** Weekly reports, monthly growth review, conversion analysis, updated campaign plan and next-month backlog.

## First campaign
**Title:** “Your Business Is Growing. Can Your Systems Keep Up?”

**Purpose:** Help business owners recognise operational visibility and coordination challenges, then invite them to a live Avenize demonstration.

**Message:** Growing businesses need a clearer view of what is happening across their operations. Avenize is designed to help business owners understand the state of their business from one environment.

**CTA:** Request a product demonstration. Use only after the live demo path and contact destination are verified.

**Activation format:** Short educational presentation, guided discussion, verified live product walkthrough, questions and opt-in follow-up. No paid venue, catering, media or subscription is assumed.

## Zero-budget acquisition
- Organic publishing using approved existing channels.
- Direct, individually researched business outreach.
- Founder and team networks, with permission and no implied endorsement.
- Partner-hosted workshops and business community sessions.
- Referral arrangements only after written commercial approval.
- QR-led opt-in demonstrations.
- Reuse and adapt one strong approved creative concept across formats.
- Earned media and collaboration opportunities.

Do not scrape or purchase cold lists. Do not automate unsolicited bulk messages. Respect platform rules, privacy and opt-out requests.

## Weekly operating rhythm
- Monday: confirm priorities, approvals, production and outreach targets.
- Daily: update asset status, prospect next actions and blockers.
- Wednesday: review creative quality, product claims and pipeline health.
- Friday: issue concise performance report with evidence, outcomes, risks and next-week actions.
- Month-end: review commercial conversion, segment quality, partner contribution and reinvestment recommendation.

## Reporting definitions
Track researched businesses, contacted prospects, qualified leads, replies, meetings, demonstrations, registrations, activated users, paying customers, realised revenue, partnership discussions and signed agreements. Report reach and engagement as supporting indicators only.

## Completion rule
Do not mark a deliverable complete because a draft exists. Record source file, final format, owner, due date, approval status, delivery/publication evidence and next commercial action.
