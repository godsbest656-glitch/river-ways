## Publishing rules

- One primary creative concept may be adapted across channels; do not mechanically post identical copy everywhere.
- Do not publish a feature demonstration until the exact workflow has been verified in the live product.
- Do not claim customer feedback, adoption, results or testimonials without evidence and permission.
- Every CTA needs a tested destination and tracking source.
- Do not publish workshop dates, venues, speakers or partner logos until confirmed.
- Record publication URL, date/time, format, owner and early performance.
- Respect platform policies, consent and opt-out requests.

## Weekly review

Compare channels by qualified replies, demo requests and lead quality, not just likes or views. Record which audience, problem statement, format and CTA generated the strongest commercial signal.
