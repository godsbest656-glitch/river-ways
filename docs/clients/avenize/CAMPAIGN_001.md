# Campaign 001 — Your Business Is Growing. Can Your Systems Keep Up?

**Client:** Avenize  
**Agency:** River Ways  
**Campaign status:** Creative concept and copy direction ready for internal review; product proof, CTA destination and client approval pending.  
**Budget:** ₦0 assumed.  
**Primary market:** Lagos, Nigeria.

## 1. Campaign purpose

Introduce Avenize to business owners and decision-makers through a recognisable operational problem: as a business grows, it becomes harder to maintain a clear view of what is happening across teams and business functions.

The campaign should create qualified interest in seeing the actual Avenize product. It must not promise outcomes that have not been verified.

## 2. Audience

Primary: SME owners, founders, directors and operations leaders in Lagos.

Secondary: professional services, agencies and consultancies, retail/distribution businesses, growing technology companies and business advisers.

## 3. Core message

**Campaign headline:** Your Business Is Growing. Can Your Systems Keep Up?

**Product promise:** The state of your business in seconds.

**Brand line:** Operate. Grow. Lead.

**Supporting message:** As a business grows, keeping track of people, work and day-to-day operations can become harder. Avenize is a Business Operating System designed to help business owners understand and manage their operations from one environment.

**Primary CTA:** Request an Avenize demonstration.

Use the CTA only after the official destination, contact route and demonstration process have been confirmed.

## 4. Message architecture

- Tension: Growth introduces more moving parts.
- Recognition: Business owners need a clearer view of what is happening.
- Reframe: Better operational visibility begins with a connected view of the business.
- Product introduction: Avenize is a Business Operating System.
- Action: See the actual product in a guided demonstration.

Avoid fear-based language suggesting the business is failing. The tone is confident, respectful and practical.

## 8. Product demonstration video 02

Do not script a feature-by-feature walkthrough until a product audit establishes what works. First record a guided session with the product owner:
- Identify the actual starting screen and user role.
- Select 2–3 verified workflows that solve a real business-owner question.
- Capture the exact sequence using safe demo data.
- Explain what the viewer sees and why it matters.
- Include limitations or setup requirements where relevant.
- End with a verified next step.

## 9. Channel adaptation

- LinkedIn: executive framing, business operations insight, founder/owner perspective and demonstration CTA.
- Instagram: strong visual hook, concise carousel and caption, story cutdowns.
- Facebook: practical SME problem/solution explanation and event/community distribution.
- TikTok: human-led short video with immediate hook and captions.
- YouTube: product walkthrough and short clips, once genuine footage is available.
- X: concise insight thread and product introduction.
- WhatsApp Business: permission-based direct introduction, compact image and clear reply path.

No bulk unsolicited WhatsApp distribution. Use warm introductions, opted-in contacts and respectful one-to-one outreach.
