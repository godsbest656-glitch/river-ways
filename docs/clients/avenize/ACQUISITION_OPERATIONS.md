# Avenize — Lead acquisition and conversion operations

**Status:** Operating design ready; live CRM workspace and product CTA are not yet connected.
**Market:** Lagos first, Nigeria-wide research as capacity allows.
**Budget:** Organic and direct outreach only unless approved otherwise.

## 1. Funnel stages

| Stage | Entry condition | Required next action | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Researched | Business fits target segment and a public business contact route is identified | Record source, segment and reason for fit | Research complete |
| Contact ready | Contact route is relevant and lawful to use | Prepare a personalised, contextual message | Message reviewed |
| Contacted | First approved message sent | Record channel, date and follow-up due date | Reply or follow-up due |
| Engaged | Prospect replies or expresses interest | Understand business context and permission to continue | Qualified or disqualified |
| Qualified | Fits target, has an operational need, decision influence and willingness to explore | Offer a demonstration | Demo booked or nurture |
| Demo booked | Prospect confirms a date/time | Send confirmation and prepare relevant verified workflow | Demo completed/cancelled |
| Demo completed | Demonstration occurred | Record needs, questions, objections and agreed next step | Trial/registration, proposal or nurture |
| Evaluation | Prospect is actively assessing Avenize | Answer only verified product/pricing questions | Won, lost or nurture |
| Registered | Account registration is confirmed | Confirm onboarding and activation path | Activated or stalled |
| Activated | User completes the agreed meaningful first-use event | Check adoption and support needs | Customer or nurture |
| Customer | Paying status and realised payment are confirmed | Onboarding, retention and referral process | Ongoing customer |
| Nurture | Fit exists but timing/readiness is not immediate | Record reason and permission-based next contact date | Re-engaged or closed |
| Disqualified | Poor fit, no permission, invalid contact or explicit opt-out | Record minimal reason; suppress further outreach where required | Closed |

Do not skip stages to inflate conversion. Registration is not activation; a verbal intention is not revenue; a proposal is not a customer.

## 2. Minimum lead record

- Lead ID.
- Business name and public website/profile.
- Segment and operating location.
- Public business contact route and source URL.
- Contact person and role, only where publicly available or supplied with permission.
- Research date and researcher.
- Reason for fit and observed business context.
- Stated operational need (prospect's own words where possible).
- Lead source/campaign/referrer.
- Consent/permission status and opt-out status.
- Current stage, owner and next action date.
- Last contact date/channel and concise interaction history.
- Demo status/date and verified workflow of interest.
- Registration/activation status, if confirmed.
- Customer/payment status and realised revenue, if confirmed.
- Disqualification/nurture reason.

Collect only what is needed to qualify and manage the relationship. Do not collect sensitive personal data or infer private characteristics.

## 3. Qualification rubric

A lead is qualified only when there is evidence for:
1. **Fit:** Business matches one of Avenize's agreed target segments.
2. **Need:** Prospect describes a real operational visibility or coordination problem.
3. **Influence:** Contact is a decision-maker or can introduce one.
4. **Interest:** Prospect agrees to explore the product or a demonstration.
5. **Readiness:** A realistic next step and timeframe exist.

Record evidence and unknowns. Do not infer budget, authority or urgency from job title alone.

## 4. Outreach sequence

- Day 0: Personalised introduction referencing a relevant public business context; state why Avenize may be relevant.
- Day 3–5: One useful follow-up with a concise question; do not resend the same pitch.
- Day 8–12: Share an approved educational asset or offer a demonstration if interest exists.
- Thereafter: Stop unless the prospect has invited further contact or an appropriate permission-based nurture cadence is agreed.

Immediately honour opt-outs and requests not to be contacted. No purchased cold lists, scraping of private contact data, bulk unsolicited WhatsApp messages or automated spam.

## 5. Message templates

### First introduction
Hello [Name], I came across [Business] while researching [relevant public context]. I'm reaching out from the Avenize team. Avenize is a Business Operating System designed to help business owners understand and manage their operations from one environment. Its promise is “The state of your business in seconds.” If operational visibility is something your team is exploring, would a short introduction or product demonstration be useful?

### Follow-up
Hello [Name], following up on my note about Avenize. I thought the idea might be relevant if keeping a clear view across business operations is becoming more demanding as the company grows. Would you like me to send a short overview, or would a demonstration be more useful?

### Demo confirmation
Hello [Name], thank you for agreeing to explore Avenize. Your demonstration is scheduled for [date/time] via [confirmed channel]. We'll focus on [verified workflow or business question]. Please let me know if there is a particular operational challenge you'd like us to address. [Verified meeting link]

### Post-demo follow-up
Thank you for taking the time to see Avenize. From our discussion, the priorities you raised were [prospect-confirmed points]. The next step we agreed is [action] by [date]. Please reply if I have missed or misunderstood anything.

All placeholders must be completed from verified information. Do not send until product facts, sender identity and CTA destination are approved.

## 6. Demo operating procedure

Before:
- Confirm attendee, role, business context, time and channel.
- Ask which operational question they most want answered.
- Prepare a safe demo account with non-sensitive sample data.
- Test access and only select verified, working product workflows.

During:
- Start with the prospect's business question.
- Show the real interface and actual workflow.
- Explain limitations honestly.
- Capture questions and feedback with permission.
- Agree one next action.

After:
- Send the agreed follow-up.
- Update CRM stage and next-action date.
- Record objections and product gaps without promising roadmap delivery.
- Attribute the opportunity to its source/campaign.

## 7. Weekly performance report

Report counts and rates with denominator definitions:
- Businesses researched.
- Valid contact routes identified.
- Prospects contacted.
- Replies and positive replies.
- Qualified leads.
- Demos booked / completed / no-show.
- Registrations / activated users.
- Paying customers and realised revenue.
- Lead-to-demo and demo-to-customer conversion.
- Median days between stages.
- Disqualification and opt-out reasons.
- Partnership-sourced leads and conversions.

Break down by segment, source, campaign and owner where sample sizes are meaningful. Never report a rate without its numerator and denominator.

## 8. CRM implementation gate

Avenize's CRM destination has not been specified. Do not mix Avenize prospects into River Ways' own sales pipeline by default. Before connecting a CRM, confirm:
- Client-owned workspace/account and administrator.
- Data controller/processor roles and access permissions.
- Required fields and retention rules.
- Consent and suppression handling.
- Export/backup and deletion process.
- Reporting owner and review cadence.

Until that is confirmed, use an approved client-owned spreadsheet or CRM workspace; do not create a shadow database with personal data in the River Ways public repository.
