# Avenize — Lagos business-owner workshop activation

**Working title:** Your Business Is Growing. Can Your Systems Keep Up?  
**Status:** Proposed activation; no event is booked, funded or publicly announced.  
**Market:** Lagos, Nigeria.  
**Budget:** ₦0 assumed; use an existing host, community or virtual session unless a cost is approved.

## Objective

Create a practical educational session that helps SME owners recognise the operational visibility challenges that come with growth, introduces Avenize accurately and gives interested participants an opportunity to see a verified product demonstration.

## Audience

- SME owners, founders and directors.
- Operations leads and business managers.
- Professional services, agencies, retail/distribution and growing technology businesses.

Invite for relevance, not raw attendee volume.

## Proposed 60-minute run of show

| Time | Segment | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Welcome, context and consent/housekeeping | Host |
| 5–15 min | The growing-business visibility challenge | Facilitator |
| 15–25 min | Interactive business-owner discussion | Facilitator |
| 25–40 min | Avenize introduction and verified live demonstration | Avenize product presenter |
| 40–50 min | Questions, limitations and practical fit | Avenize presenter |
| 50–57 min | Optional demo follow-up and next steps | Growth lead |
| 57–60 min | Close and feedback | Host |

## Discussion prompts

- What becomes harder to keep track of as the business grows?
- Where do people currently go to understand what is happening?
- Which decisions are delayed because information is fragmented?
- What would a clearer operational picture help the business owner do?

These are discussion prompts, not claims that every attendee has the same problem.

## No-budget activation model

- Ask an existing business community, coworking space, association or professional network to host.
- Offer a useful educational session, not a sales-only event.
- Use an online meeting if no suitable no-cost venue exists.
- Promote through the host's approved channels and Avenize's organic channels.
- Use a simple opt-in registration form with a clear privacy notice and separate consent for follow-up.
- Capture only attendance and information required for the requested follow-up.
- Use a tested QR code linked to the confirmed registration or demo destination.

No venue, refreshments, travel, printing, paid promotion, speaker fee or software subscription is assumed.

## Registration fields

Minimum: name, business name, business role, business email or chosen contact route, business category, city, and optional question about the operational topic they want addressed.

Explain who will use the information, why it is collected and how to opt out. Do not add attendees to marketing lists by default.

## Product demonstration readiness

Before the event:
- Confirm Avenize product owner and presenter.
- Select 2–3 workflows that are working in the live product.
- Prepare a safe demo account and test the full sequence.
- Avoid sensitive customer data and fabricated screenshots.
- Confirm product limitations and unanswered questions.
- Test screen sharing, audio and fallback recording.
- Agree the exact CTA and follow-up route.

If no verified live workflow is available, replace the demo with an honest product preview and do not present a prototype as a live feature.

## Follow-up

Within 1–2 business days:
- Thank attendees.
- Follow up only with those who opted in.
- Record the question or use case they asked to explore.
- Offer a demo where appropriate.
- Update acquisition pipeline stages and next actions.
- Report attendance, opt-in leads, qualified leads, demos and registrations separately.

## Success measures

Qualified attendees, opt-in leads, demos booked/completed, registrations, activated users, paying customers and feedback quality. Attendance and social reach are supporting indicators only.
