# Avenize × LIMBsimple — SME growth and distribution concept

**Status:** Private discussion draft. Not an offer, endorsement, agreement or commitment.
**Prepared by:** River Ways Agency for Avenize review.
**Prospect:** LIMBsimple.
**Purpose:** Explore a commercial growth and distribution relationship connecting Avenize with relevant SMEs through education, referrals and product demonstrations.

## 1. The opportunity

Avenize is a Business Operating System designed to help business owners understand and manage their operations from one environment. Its promise is: **“The state of your business in seconds.”**

The Avenize brief identifies LIMBsimple as a potential SME access and distribution partner. This proposal explores a practical pilot based on workshops, referrals, demonstrations and measurable customer acquisition.

No assumptions are made about LIMBsimple's current customer base, distribution reach, commercial model or willingness to participate.

## 2. Proposed collaboration areas

- **SME access:** Explore relevant business communities and opt-in SME audiences.
- **Business workshops:** Co-host practical sessions on operational visibility and business growth.
- **Referrals:** Introduce interested business owners through a transparent, permission-based process.
- **Product demonstrations:** Arrange guided Avenize demonstrations for qualified businesses.
- **Education:** Share approved business-owner resources and product explainers.
- **Feedback:** Collect structured feedback from participating SMEs, with consent.

## 3. Mutual value hypothesis

**Potential value to LIMBsimple**
- An additional relevant business solution to introduce to interested SMEs.
- Educational programming for its business community.
- Potential commercial referral income if separately agreed.
- Stronger engagement through practical workshops and demonstrations.

**Potential value to Avenize**
- Access to relevant SME communities with permission.
- Qualified introductions and demonstrations.
- Lower-cost distribution through trusted business networks.
- Market feedback on product fit and onboarding.

These are hypotheses to validate together, not guaranteed outcomes.

## 4. Proposed 30-day pilot

**Working activation:** “Your Business Is Growing. Can Your Systems Keep Up?”

Pilot components:
1. Agree one target SME segment and audience invitation process.
2. Co-design a 45–60 minute educational session.
3. Invite interested businesses through approved channels.
4. Present operational visibility challenges in plain language.
5. Demonstrate only verified Avenize product workflows.
6. Offer an opt-in next step for attendees who request follow-up.
7. Review lead quality, attendance, demos and registrations together.

Start online or with an existing partner-hosted venue to avoid unapproved event costs.

## 5. Roles to agree

**Avenize**
- Product demonstration and accurate product information.
- Demo access, onboarding route and customer support.
- Approved campaign copy, product assets and CTA.
- Lead follow-up and commercial conversion reporting.

**LIMBsimple**
- Confirm audience fit and invitation route.
- Agree distribution channels and event role.
- Obtain permission for attendee follow-up and data transfer.
- Provide agreed event or referral reporting.

**River Ways**
- Campaign concept and creative production.
- Workshop presentation and promotional materials.
- Registration and opt-in journey design.
- Lead attribution, reporting and follow-up coordination.

All responsibilities remain proposed until accepted in writing.

## 6. Proposed success measures

- Number of eligible businesses reached through approved channels.
- Registrations and attendance.
- Opt-in leads and qualified SME opportunities.
- Demonstrations booked and completed.
- Product registrations and activated users.
- Paying customers and realised revenue.
- Referral-to-demo and demo-to-customer conversion.
- Feedback and cost per qualified opportunity.

Set numeric targets after audience size and product onboarding capacity are confirmed.

## 7. Commercial structures for discussion

Possible structures, subject to separate agreement:
- No-cost pilot with a joint review.
- Fixed-scope sponsored workshop.
- Transparent referral fee per agreed qualifying event.
- Revenue share on collected revenue from attributed customers.
- Co-funded activation with pre-approved budgets and cost caps.

No referral fee, percentage, payment timing, exclusivity or financial commitment is proposed as agreed. Any commercial arrangement must define attribution window, qualifying event, refunds, reporting, payment schedule, tax treatment and termination.

## 8. Next steps

1. Avenize approves the concept for discussion.

3. Both parties confirm target audience, distribution rights and consent flow.
4. Agree pilot format, roles, cost ceiling, data responsibilities and KPIs.
5. Sign written terms before referral payments, co-branding or event commitments.
6. Run the pilot and review evidence before scaling.
