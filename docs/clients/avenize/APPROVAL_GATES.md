# Avenize — Approval and evidence gates

## Product truth gate
Classify every product statement as:
- Verified and demonstrable now.
- Available but not yet demonstrated.
- Planned or under development.
- Unknown / not approved for publication.

Only verified claims may appear as current product capabilities. Use genuine screenshots and approved demo data.

## Brand gate
- Exact supplied logo.
- Correct sampled palette and approved extensions.
- No unapproved redesign or vector tracing.
- Correct separation between Avenize and River Ways.
- Legible, accessible and platform-appropriate composition.

## Commercial gate
- Pricing and availability confirmed.
- Registration/demo destination tested.
- Contact details confirmed.
- No unapproved commission, discount, partnership, funding or exclusivity promise.
- No paid spend or subscription without written approval.

## Publication gate
- Audience and campaign objective documented.
- Copy, visual and CTA reviewed.
- Links and tracking tested.
- Client approval recorded.
- Correct platform format and publishing date.
- Published URL/screenshot and performance baseline recorded.

## Partnership gate
Niyi Adesanya and LIMBsimple are prospects. Proposals must describe potential mutual value, proposed activities, measurable outcomes and open terms. Do not imply endorsement, agreement, commitment or existing relationship.

## Financial gate
Any revenue-sharing, referral reward, marketing reinvestment, recoupable advance, sponsored activation or partner-funded event requires written Avenize approval before representation or commitment.
