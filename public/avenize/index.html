Avenize is an AI-powered Business Operating System in development: a connected operating layer designed to help a business understand itself, coordinate its people and work, and act with better context.

The idea

One business.
Connected context.

Businesses rarely operate in one place. Customer relationships, people, responsibilities, decisions, workflows and performance signals are spread across tools and teams. The gaps between them make it harder to see what is happening and what needs attention.

Avenize is being shaped around that reality. Its ambition is to connect the context behind business activity—so people can understand the whole picture, coordinate work and make better-informed decisions.

This is a product direction, not a claim that every module or integration is already built.

System map / 01—06

Six connected
operating areas.

These are the planned capability areas guiding the product architecture. Their availability, scope and integrations will be confirmed as each part is designed, built and verified.

01 / IDENTITY

Business identity & graph

A connected model of the organisation, its entities, relationships, roles, resources and operating context.

02 / CUSTOMERS

Customer relationships

CRM context for accounts, contacts, conversations, opportunities, activity and follow-through.

03 / PEOPLE

People & operations

People, roles, responsibilities and HR-adjacent workflows with appropriate privacy and access controls.

04 / ASSISTANCE

AI assistant

Context-aware assistance to help authorised users understand information, plan work and take permitted actions.

05 / PULSE

Pulse & intelligence

Useful views of activity, progress, exceptions, trends and decisions that need attention.

06 / EXTENSIONS

Marketplace & workflows

Approved tools, connected extensions and repeatable processes that help work move across the business.

Product boundary

Built to connect.
Designed to respect.

A separate product

Avenize has its own product identity, roadmap, application architecture, data model and release process. It is not part of the River Ways marketing website.

Permission-aware by design

Business and people data require clear ownership, role-based access, privacy controls, retention rules and auditable actions.

Human accountability

AI assistance must respect user permissions, explain its context, preserve provenance and request confirmation for consequential actions.

Honest release status

Avenize is in development. No public sign-up, product workspace, live integrations, pricing or launch date is being offered here.
