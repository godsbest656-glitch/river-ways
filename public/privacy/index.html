Privacy notice

Last reviewed: 12 October 2026

This notice explains how River Ways handles information you choose to submit through the website enquiry form.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, we process your name and email address, and any company, business goal or project context you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and manage a potential business relationship. Please do not submit passwords, financial account details, sensitive personal information or confidential third-party material.

Storage and abuse prevention

When the enquiry service is configured, submitted enquiries are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database operated for River Ways. The endpoint uses a short-window abuse-prevention check based on a hash of the connecting IP address rather than storing the raw address for that check. Enquiry storage is disabled until the required database binding and a strong rate-limit secret are configured.

Email notifications

If email notification is configured, enquiry details are sent through Resend to River Ways’ designated business inbox. A notification can fail even when an enquiry has been saved, so the operator must review stored enquiries and notification status and follow up appropriately. Do not assume a submission has been received unless the form confirms success.

Retention and requests

River Ways must configure and periodically review retention, deletion, access controls and backup policies for the enquiry database and notification provider. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of enquiry information, contact oluwafemi@riverwayse.com. Requests are handled subject to applicable law.

Analytics and third parties

This notice does not claim that analytics or social-listening services are active. If additional services are introduced, this notice should be updated to explain what information is collected, why it is processed, which providers are involved, and any consent choices required by applicable law.
