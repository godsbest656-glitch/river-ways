Privacy notice

Last reviewed: 9 October 2026

This notice explains how River Ways handles information you choose to submit through the website enquiry form.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, we process your name and email address, and any company, business goal or project context you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and manage a potential business relationship. Please do not submit passwords, financial account details, sensitive personal information or confidential third-party material.

Security and abuse prevention

The form service uses a short-window abuse-prevention check. It stores a hash of the connecting IP address for rate limiting rather than intentionally storing the raw address in the enquiry record. Website enquiries are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database configured by the site operator. Appropriate access, retention, deletion and backup settings must be maintained by the operator.

Email notifications and service providers

If configured, an enquiry notification is sent through Resend to River Ways' designated business inbox. These providers process information under their applicable service terms and privacy policies. If the service is not configured or temporarily unavailable, the form may not be able to accept your enquiry; you can email oluwafemi@riverwayse.com.

Retention and your requests

Enquiry information should be kept only as long as needed for follow-up, business records and applicable legal requirements. Contact oluwafemi@riverwayse.com to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable law.

Analytics and demand intelligence

This notice does not claim that analytics or social-listening integrations are currently active. If such services are introduced, this notice should be updated to describe the information collected, purposes, providers and any consent choices required by applicable law.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website and its integrations evolve. The review date above indicates the latest stated revision.
