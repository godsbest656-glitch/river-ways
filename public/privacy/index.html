Privacy notice

Draft notice for the River Ways website. Review the service-provider and retention details before enabling live enquiry submission.

Please note: Do not include passwords, payment details, health information or other sensitive personal information in a general project enquiry.

Information you provide

If you submit a project enquiry, the site is designed to process your name, email address, company or brand, selected project goal and any context you choose to include. Please share only information needed for us to understand your request.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to review and respond to your request, determine whether our services may be suitable, and manage any resulting business conversation. We do not need your diagnostic answers to provide the on-page growth readiness summary; that self-reflection tool runs in your browser and does not send those answers to River Ways.

How enquiry information is handled

The planned direct enquiry feature uses Cloudflare Workers to receive and protect the request and Resend to deliver the message to River Ways. Direct submission should remain disabled until provider configuration, sender verification, security checks and this notice have been reviewed. Providers may process technical data needed to deliver and protect their services.

Retention and sharing

Enquiry information should be kept only as long as needed to respond and manage the resulting business relationship, subject to any applicable legal recordkeeping obligations. Access should be limited to people who need it. River Ways should not sell enquiry information. Information may be processed by service providers acting to host or deliver the site and its messages.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

The current website should not add non-essential analytics, advertising pixels, session recording or other tracking tools without first documenting the purpose, provider, configuration and applicable notice or consent requirements. Browser storage and third-party resource use should be reviewed before launch.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other handling of personal information. To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact oluwafemi@riverwayse.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to restrict access and prevent misuse, loss or unauthorised disclosure. Internet transmission and email cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure; please avoid sending confidential or highly sensitive information through a general enquiry form.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the site's services, data flows, providers or legal requirements change. The published version should include an accurate effective or reviewed date.
