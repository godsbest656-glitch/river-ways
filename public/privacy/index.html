River Ways / Privacy

A clear note on your information.

This notice explains what happens when you send River Ways a project enquiry through this website.

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

What you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, company or brand, the area you want to improve, and any optional context you provide. When available, the website also records the landing path, referring page and campaign tags (UTM parameters) to help us understand how an enquiry arrived. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information or confidential third-party information.

Why we use it

We use the information to review your enquiry, respond to you, assess whether our services are a fit, and maintain a record of business communications. We do not sell enquiry details to advertisers.

Service providers and security

Enquiries are intended to be stored in a secured Cloudflare D1 database and notification emails are intended to be sent through Resend when those services are configured. The endpoint uses basic validation, rate limiting and a salted hash for abuse prevention. The lead record includes an initial follow-up stage and notification status so the team can manage responses. We do not intentionally store the raw client IP in the lead record. Do not treat a successful website build as proof that these production services are configured; the live enquiry process must be tested before launch.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship, and meet applicable obligations. You may ask to access, correct or delete information you submitted, subject to applicable requirements.

Contact

For questions about this notice or an enquiry you submitted, email akinsify0511@gmail.com.

This is a practical website privacy notice, not legal advice. River Ways should review it against its actual data practices and applicable privacy obligations before production launch.
