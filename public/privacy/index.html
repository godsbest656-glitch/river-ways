River Ways / Privacy

A clear note on your information.

This notice explains how River Ways handles information when you visit this website or send a project enquiry.

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

What you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, company or brand, the area you want to improve, and any optional context you provide. When available, the website records the landing path, the referring page's origin and path, and campaign tags (UTM parameters) to help us understand how an enquiry arrived. Query strings and URL fragments are not retained in those page references. If you complete the Growth Diagnostic and choose to continue to the enquiry form, the handoff may also carry a journey identifier, an engagement preference and campaign tags. Your diagnostic answers and score remain in your browser and are not sent with the enquiry. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information or confidential third-party information.

Why we use it

We use the information to review your enquiry, respond to you, assess whether our services are a fit, and maintain a record of business communications. We do not sell enquiry details to advertisers.

Service providers and security

Enquiries are intended to be stored in a Cloudflare D1 database and notification emails are intended to be sent through Resend when those services are configured. The endpoint uses basic validation, rate limiting and a salted hash for abuse prevention. The lead record includes an initial follow-up stage and notification status so the team can manage responses. We do not intentionally store the raw client IP in the lead record. The homepage currently requests selected photographs from the Unsplash image CDN and typefaces from Google Fonts. When those resources load, the browser makes requests to those providers, which may receive technical connection information such as an IP address and browser request metadata. Review the providers' own privacy information: Unsplash Privacy Policy and Google Fonts privacy FAQ. We do not currently add analytics or advertising pixels to this website. Do not treat a successful website build as proof that production enquiry services are configured; the live enquiry process must be tested before launch.

Retention and your choices

We need enquiry information to respond, manage the business relationship and meet applicable obligations. An automated retention or deletion schedule is not currently implemented; River Ways must establish and apply one before production lead capture. You may ask to access, correct or delete information you submitted, subject to applicable requirements.
